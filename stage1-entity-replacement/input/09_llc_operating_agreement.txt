## ARTICLE VII: BUY-SELL PROVISIONS

**7.1 Trigger Events.** The buy-sell provisions of this Article shall be triggered upon the occurrence of any of the following events with respect to a Member: (a) death; (b) permanent disability; (c) voluntary withdrawal with 90 days written notice; (d) bankruptcy filing; (e) divorce where membership interest becomes marital property subject to division; or (f) material breach of this Agreement.

**7.2 Valuation Method.** Upon a triggering event, the fair market value of the departing Member's interest shall be determined by the average of two independent appraisals conducted by certified business appraisers selected by the Company and the departing Member (or their representative). If the two appraisals differ by more than 15%, a third appraiser shall be selected jointly, and the final value shall be the average of all three appraisals.

**7.3 Purchase Terms.** The Company or remaining Members shall have the option to purchase the interest at the appraised value, payable 30% at closing and the balance over sixty (60) months at 5% annual interest.
