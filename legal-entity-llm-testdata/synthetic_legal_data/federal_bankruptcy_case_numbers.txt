**CIVIL COMPLAINT FOR DAMAGES**

Plaintiff Marcus Anthony Rodriguez, by and through his undersigned counsel, respectfully submits this Complaint against Defendant Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc., and alleges as follows:

**PARTIES AND JURISDICTION**

1. Plaintiff Marcus Anthony Rodriguez is an individual residing at 485 West 43rd Street, Apartment 12B, New York, NY 10036. His Social Security Number is 078-45-9821, and he may be reached at (212) 555-7394.

2. Defendant Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1007 North Orange Street, Suite 200, Wilmington, DE 19801.

3. This Court has subject matter jurisdiction pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1331 and diversity jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1332, as the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000 and complete diversity exists between the parties.

4. Venue is proper in this District pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1391(b) because Defendant conducts substantial business activities within this judicial district.

**FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS**

5. This action arises from Defendant's fraudulent investment scheme that resulted in substantial financial losses to Rodriguez and other similarly situated investors.

6. The fraudulent conduct described herein was also the subject of a related bankruptcy proceeding in Case No. 23-BK-10458 filed in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on September 15, 2023.

7. Prior to the federal bankruptcy case 23-BK-10458, Rodriguez had initiated proceedings in New York State Supreme Court under Case No. 2022-CV-00891, which was subsequently removed to this federal court and assigned Civil Action No. 1:23-cv-04821-ABC.

8. Beginning in January 2022, representatives of Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc. contacted Rodriguez at his residence located at 485 West 43rd Street, Apartment 12B, New York, NY 10036, soliciting him to invest in what they described as "low-risk, high-yield securities."

9. The Defendant's misconduct is currently under investigation by the Securities and Exchange Commission in SEC File No. HO-12345, which was initiated following complaints filed by Rodriguez and other investors.

10. Rodriguez, relying on the false representations made by Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc., invested $125,000 of his retirement savings between February and August 2022.

11. Unknown to the plaintiff at the time, the investment vehicle promoted by Defendant was part of a Ponzi scheme that used new investor funds to pay fictitious returns to earlier investors.

12. The administrative proceeding referenced as AP-2023-0147 before the Securities and Exchange Commission has revealed that Defendant operated without proper licenses and failed to register the securities offerings as required by federal law.

13. In connection with state court case 2022-CV-00891, Rodriguez discovered that his investment funds had been commingled with other investor monies and used for unauthorized purposes, including personal expenses of Defendant's officers.

14. He attempted to withdraw his investment in December 2022 but was informed by Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc. that his funds were "temporarily unavailable due to market conditions."

15. The SEC enforcement action SEC File No. HO-12345 has confirmed that Defendant's business model was fundamentally fraudulent and that investor funds were never actually invested as promised.

16. Rodriguez's attempts to recover his investment through the state court proceeding 2022-CV-00891 were hampered by Defendant's subsequent bankruptcy filing under case number 23-BK-10458.

17. The administrative proceeding AP-2023-0147 has established that Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc. violated multiple federal securities laws, including registration requirements and anti-fraud provisions.

18. As a direct result of Defendant's fraudulent conduct, Rodriguez has suffered actual damages of not less than $125,000, plus accrued interest and additional consequential damages.

**CAUSES OF ACTION**

**COUNT I: FEDERAL SECURITIES FRAUD**

19. Rodriguez incorporates by reference all preceding paragraphs as if fully set forth herein.

20. Defendant's conduct violates Section 10(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and Rule 10b-5 promulgated thereunder.

21. The fraudulent scheme detailed in this Complaint and in related proceedings, including federal case 1:23-cv-04821-ABC, bankruptcy case 23-BK-10458, and SEC File No. HO-12345, demonstrates a pattern of deceptive practices designed to defraud investors.

**COUNT II: COMMON LAW FRAUD**

22. Plaintiff re-alleges and incorporates by reference all preceding paragraphs.

23. Whitmore Financial Holdings, Inc. made material misrepresentations of fact to Rodriguez with the intent to induce him to invest his funds.

24. The fraudulent nature of these representations has been confirmed through multiple legal proceedings, including administrative proceeding AP-2023-0147 and the ongoing bankruptcy case 23-BK-10458.
