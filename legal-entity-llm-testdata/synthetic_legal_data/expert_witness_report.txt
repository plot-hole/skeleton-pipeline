**MEDICAL HISTORY AND PROCEDURE**

Isabella Marie Santos, a 55-year-old female, presented with severe three-vessel coronary artery disease requiring surgical intervention. She had a documented history of diabetes mellitus, hypertension, and hyperlipidemia. Prior to surgery, the patient was maintained on metoprolol 50mg twice daily among other medications.

On August 14, 2022, Dr. Thompson performed a three-vessel coronary artery bypass graft procedure on Ms. Santos. The surgery involved bypassing the left anterior descending artery, right coronary artery, and obtuse marginal branch of the circumflex artery using saphenous vein grafts.

**EXPERT OPINIONS**

Based on my review of the medical records and my expertise in cardiovascular medicine, I offer the following opinions within a reasonable degree of medical certainty:

**1. Standard of Care Violation**

Dr. Thompson's management of Isabella Santos fell below the accepted standard of care for cardiac surgery. Specifically, Thompson failed to adequately assess the patient's cardiac function prior to surgery. The preoperative echocardiogram clearly showed an ejection fraction of 25%, indicating severely compromised left ventricular function. Standard protocol requires additional hemodynamic assessment and possible placement of intra-aortic balloon pump support in patients with such severe dysfunction.

Furthermore, Dr. Thompson proceeded with surgery despite the patient's elevated troponin levels (4.8 ng/mL) indicating ongoing myocardial injury. The standard of care required delay of the procedure and medical stabilization of Isabella Santos before attempting surgical intervention.

**2. Causation Analysis**

The substandard care provided by the defendant directly caused significant harm to Ms. Santos. Thompson's failure to provide adequate cardiac support during the procedure resulted in perioperative myocardial infarction and subsequent cardiogenic shock. This complication was entirely preventable with proper preoperative planning and intraoperative management.

The patient suffered prolonged hospitalization requiring intensive care support for 18 days, resulting in additional medical expenses exceeding $287,500. She also required placement of a left ventricular assist device, adding another $145,000 in medical costs.

**3. Long-term Consequences**

Isabella Santos now suffers from permanent cardiac disability with a current ejection fraction of only 15%. She requires ongoing medical management including heart failure medications and frequent cardiology follow-up visits. The patient's functional capacity has been severely limited, and she is now classified as New York Heart Association Class III heart failure.

Ms. Santos will require consideration for heart transplantation within the next two to three years, representing additional medical expenses of approximately $500,000 to $750,000.

**CONCLUSION**

In my expert medical opinion, Dr. Michael Jonathan Thompson's care of Isabella Marie Santos on August 14, 2022, fell below the accepted standard of care for cardiac surgery. His actions directly caused preventable complications resulting in permanent cardiac disability and substantial medical expenses. The harm suffered by the patient was entirely avoidable with proper adherence to standard cardiac surgical protocols.

This opinion is rendered within a reasonable degree of medical certainty based on my training, experience, and review of the relevant medical records.
