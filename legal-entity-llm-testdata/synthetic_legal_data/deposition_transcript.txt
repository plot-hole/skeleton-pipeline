PATRICIA HELEN RODRIGUEZ, having been first duly sworn, was examined and testified as follows:

**EXAMINATION BY MS. CHEN:**

Q. Please state your full name and address for the record.

A. My name is Patricia Helen Rodriguez. I live at 2847 North Halsted Street, Chicago, Illinois 60657.

Q. Ms. Rodriguez, what is your phone number and email address?

A. My phone is (312) 555-9823 and my email is p.rodriguez@northsidetech.com.

Q. How do you know the parties in this case?

A. I worked directly with both companies on the contract at issue. I was the project coordinator between Meridian Logistics Corp. and Pinnacle Supply Solutions, Inc.

Q. Who did you work with specifically at each company?

A. At Meridian, I worked primarily with the CEO, Thomas Bradley Hutchinson. Mr. Hutchinson was very hands-on with this deal. At Pinnacle, my main contact was their COO, Rebecca Marie Santos.

Q. Let's talk about the contract execution. When was the agreement signed?

A. The contract was executed on March 15, 2022. I remember because it was right after the quarterly board meeting.

Q. Were you present when Mr. Hutchinson signed the contract?

A. Yes, I was in his office. He reviewed the terms one final time before signing. Thomas was particularly concerned about the delivery schedules.

Q. Did he express any concerns to you?

A. Mr. Hutchinson mentioned that Ms. Santos had assured him that Pinnacle could meet all deadlines. He said Rebecca had personally guaranteed the timeline during their February meeting.

Q. Let me show you what's been marked as Exhibit A. Do you recognize this document?

A. Yes, this is the original supply agreement between the companies. The contract value was $2,847,500.

Q. When did the alleged breach occur?

A. The breach happened on August 22, 2022. That's when Pinnacle missed their first major delivery deadline.

Q. How did you learn about the breach?

A. Thomas called me immediately. He was furious because Ms. Santos hadn't returned his calls for three days. Mr. Hutchinson said he needed me to document everything for potential legal action.

Q. What did you document?

A. I kept detailed records of all communications. Rebecca finally called back on August 25th, but by then the damage was done.

**EXAMINATION BY MR. DAVIDSON:**

Q. Ms. Rodriguez, you mentioned Ms. Santos assured Mr. Hutchinson about deadlines. Were you present for those conversations?

A. Not all of them. Some discussions were between Thomas and Rebecca directly.

Q. So you don't have firsthand knowledge of what she actually said?

A. Correct. I only know what Mr. Hutchinson told me afterward.

Q. Looking at Exhibit C-2, do you see your initials on this amendment?

A. Yes, those are my initials. This was an amendment that Thomas requested in May 2022.

Q. Did Ms. Santos object to this amendment?

A. Not initially. Rebecca seemed agreeable when we first discussed it. But later, she said her team had concerns about the modified timeline.

Q. When you say "later," can you be more specific?

A. This was in June. Ms. Santos called Thomas directly, and he relayed the conversation to me. According to him, Rebecca was now saying the changes would impact their ability to perform.

Q. Did Mr. Hutchinson document his concerns about Ms. Santos' changing position?

A. Yes. Thomas sent several emails to Rebecca between June and August. He was trying to get clarity on their position.

Q. Were you copied on those emails?

A. Some of them. Mr. Hutchinson wanted me to track all correspondence for the project file.

Q. In your opinion, did both parties understand their obligations under the contract?

A. I believe so. Thomas certainly understood what Meridian was expecting, and Rebecca seemed to grasp what Pinnacle needed to deliver.

Q. But things changed?

A. Yes. By July, Ms. Santos was raising issues that hadn't come up before. She claimed there were supply chain problems, but Thomas felt these were excuses.

Q. Did you personally witness any conversations between Mr. Hutchinson and Ms. Santos about these problems?

A. I was present for one phone call in late July. Thomas put the call on speaker. Rebecca was explaining the delays, but he wasn't satisfied with her explanations.

Q. What specifically did she say during that call?

A. Ms. Santos mentioned material shortages and shipping delays. But Mr. Hutchinson pointed out that these issues should have been anticipated when she originally committed to the timeline.

Q. How did that conversation end?

A. Thomas told Rebecca that Meridian was depending on timely performance and that any delays could result in significant damages. She said she understood, but obviously things didn't improve.
