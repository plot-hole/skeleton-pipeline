**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND GENERAL RELEASE**

This Settlement Agreement and General Release ("Agreement") is entered into on December 15, 2023, between Marcus Jonathan Bellwood, an individual residing at 847 Maple Grove Drive, Cherry Hill, New Jersey 08003, Social Security Number 456-78-9123 ("Plaintiff" or "the Claimant"), and Precision Industrial Technologies Corp., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2901 Corporate Center Boulevard, Austin, Texas 78731, EIN 74-2883497 ("Defendant" or "Company").

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, this matter arises from Civil Action No. 2023-CV-08742 filed in the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey, captioned Marcus Jonathan Bellwood v. Precision Industrial Technologies Corp., et al.;

WHEREAS, Plaintiff alleges he sustained serious personal injuries on March 8, 2022, while operating Defendant's Model XR-450 industrial press brake at his workplace, resulting in crushing injuries to his left hand and forearm;

WHEREAS, Mr. Bellwood claims that the Company's product contained design defects and inadequate safety warnings that caused his injuries;

WHEREAS, Defendant denies all liability and maintains that its product was properly designed, manufactured, and labeled;

WHEREAS, the parties participated in mediation conducted by retired Judge Patricia R. Morrison on November 28, 2023, and desire to resolve this matter without further litigation;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. SETTLEMENT PAYMENT**

Defendant agrees to pay Plaintiff the sum of One Million Three Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($1,350,000.00) in full settlement of all claims. Payment shall be made according to the following schedule:

(a) Initial payment of Four Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($450,000.00) within thirty (30) days of execution of this Agreement;
(b) Second payment of Four Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($450,000.00) on or before June 15, 2024;
(c) Final payment of Four Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($450,000.00) on or before December 15, 2024.

All payments shall be made by certified check payable to "Marcus Jonathan Bellwood" and delivered to his counsel, Robert M. Davidson, Esq., of Davidson & Associates Law Group, 1567 Commerce Street, Suite 400, Newark, New Jersey 07102.

**2. GENERAL RELEASE**

In consideration of the settlement payment, the Claimant hereby releases and forever discharges Precision Industrial Technologies Corp., its officers, directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns from any and all claims, demands, damages, actions, or causes of action arising from or related to the incident of March 8, 2022, and the use of the Model XR-450 press brake. This release shall become effective on January 15, 2024 ("Release Date").

**3. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Mr. Bellwood agrees that the terms of this settlement, including the settlement amount, shall remain strictly confidential. He shall not disclose the terms to any third party except his immediate family, accountant, tax advisor, or as required by law. Any breach of this confidentiality provision shall result in liquidated damages of One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00).

**4. NON-ADMISSION**

This Agreement shall not be construed as an admission of liability, fault, or wrongdoing by Defendant. The Company maintains that its product was not defective and that it bears no responsibility for Plaintiff's alleged injuries.

**5. ATTORNEY AUTHORIZATION**

Plaintiff is represented by Robert M. Davidson, Esq. (phone: (973) 555-0847, email: rdavidson@davidsonlaw.com) of Davidson & Associates Law Group. Defendant is represented by its General Counsel, Jennifer L. Hartwell, Esq. (phone: (512) 555-0293, email: jhartwell@precisionindustrial.com).

**6. ENTIRE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements. No modification shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

**7. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey.
