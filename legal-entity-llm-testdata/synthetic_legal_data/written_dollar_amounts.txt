**SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND GENERAL RELEASE**

**CASE NO. 2023-CV-08947**

This Settlement Agreement and General Release ("Agreement") is entered into this 15th day of November, 2023, between MARCUS J. WELLINGTON ("Plaintiff"), an individual residing at 2847 Oakwood Drive, McLean, Virginia 22102, and MERIDIAN MANUFACTURING CORPORATION ("Defendant"), a Maryland corporation with its principal place of business located at 9600 Industrial Boulevard, Baltimore, Maryland 21224.

**RECITALS**

WHEREAS, on March 8, 2022, Plaintiff sustained injuries in an industrial accident at Defendant's manufacturing facility; and

WHEREAS, Plaintiff filed a lawsuit against Defendant in the Circuit Court of Fairfax County, Virginia, Case No. 2023-CV-08947, alleging negligence, premises liability, and violations of workplace safety regulations; and

WHEREAS, Defendant denies all allegations of wrongdoing and liability but desires to resolve this matter without the expense and uncertainty of continued litigation; and

WHEREAS, both parties wish to settle all claims and disputes arising from or related to the incident of March 8, 2022.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the parties agree as follows:

**1. SETTLEMENT PAYMENT**

Defendant agrees to pay Plaintiff the total sum of Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($750,000.00) ("Settlement Amount") according to the following payment schedule:

a) First installment of Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000.00) due on December 1, 2023;
b) Second installment of One Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($150,000.00) due on February 15, 2024;
c) Third and final installment of Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($400,000.00) due on May 1, 2024.

All payments shall be made by certified check or wire transfer to the trust account of Wellington's counsel, HARRISON & ASSOCIATES, P.C., located at 1455 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Suite 800, Washington, D.C. 20004.

**2. DISTRIBUTION OF SETTLEMENT PROCEEDS**

From the Settlement Amount, the following distributions shall be made:

a) Attorney fees in the amount of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000.00) shall be paid to HARRISON & ASSOCIATES, P.C.;
b) Outstanding medical lien held by Fairfax Regional Medical Center in the amount of Sixty-Three Thousand Four Hundred Twelve Dollars and 87/100 ($63,412.87) shall be satisfied;
c) The remaining balance shall be distributed to Wellington.

**3. GENERAL RELEASE**

In consideration for the Settlement Amount, Plaintiff hereby releases and forever discharges Meridian Manufacturing Corporation, its officers, directors, employees, agents, insurers, and affiliates from any and all claims, demands, damages, or causes of action arising from or related to the incident of March 8, 2022. This release includes, but is not limited to, claims for personal injury, property damage, lost wages, pain and suffering, and punitive damages.

**4. CONFIDENTIALITY**

The parties agree that the terms of this Agreement shall remain confidential. Neither party may disclose the existence or terms of this settlement to any third party, except as required by law or to professional advisors bound by confidentiality obligations.

**5. NO ADMISSION OF LIABILITY**

This Agreement shall not be construed as an admission of liability or wrongdoing by Defendant. Meridian Manufacturing Corporation expressly denies any liability or fault in connection with the incident that forms the basis of Plaintiff's claims.

**6. ATTORNEY REPRESENTATION**

Plaintiff is represented by JENNIFER L. HARRISON, ESQ. of Harrison & Associates, P.C. Defendant is represented by ROBERT M. CHEN, ESQ. of Chen, Bradley & Partners LLP, located at 100 Light Street, Suite 2800, Baltimore, Maryland 21202.

**7. BINDING EFFECT**

This Agreement shall be binding upon the parties, their heirs, successors, and assigns. The terms herein constitute the entire agreement between the parties and may only be modified by written agreement signed by both parties.

**8. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above.
