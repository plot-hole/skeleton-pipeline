The Complainant, Detective Sarah Michelle Rodriguez, Badge No. 4721, Minneapolis Police Department, Third Precinct, 3000 Minnehaha Avenue, Minneapolis, MN 55406, being duly sworn, makes complaint to the Court and states that there is probable cause to believe that the above-named defendant committed the following offenses:

**COUNT I: CHILD ENDANGERMENT - FIRST DEGREE**
Minnesota Statute § 609.378, Subd. 1(b)(1)

**COUNT II: NEGLECT OF A CHILD**
Minnesota Statute § 626.556, Subd. 10(a)

The facts supporting a finding of probable cause are as follows:

**FACTUAL BASIS:**

On January 15, 2024, at approximately 2:30 PM, officers from the Minneapolis Police Department responded to 847 Sherburne Avenue, St. Paul, MN 55104, regarding a welfare check on two minor children. The call was initiated based on information provided by a confidential informant, hereinafter referred to as CI-1, who reported observing concerning conditions affecting the welfare of minors A.B.C., age 7, and D.E.F., age 4.

Upon arrival, officers encountered the defendant, Marcus Allen Thornfield, DOB: 03/22/1985, residing at 847 Sherburne Avenue, St. Paul, MN 55104. Thornfield appeared to be under the influence of controlled substances and was unable to provide coherent responses to basic questions regarding the children's whereabouts and condition.

The investigation revealed that Marcus Thornfield had been the sole caretaker of A.B.C. and D.E.F. for the preceding seventy-two hours while their biological mother was hospitalized. During this period, the defendant failed to provide adequate supervision, nutrition, or basic care for the children.

CI-1 provided detailed information indicating that on multiple occasions between January 12, 2024, and January 15, 2024, the children were observed outside the residence unsupervised for extended periods, including during evening hours when temperatures dropped below freezing. The older child, A.B.C., was reportedly seen attempting to care for the younger child, D.E.F., without adult supervision.

Detective Rodriguez's investigation further revealed that the defendant left the children unattended on the evening of January 14, 2024, from approximately 8:00 PM until the following morning. During this time, A.B.C. and D.E.F. were left without food, adequate heating, or any means of contacting emergency services. The older child told investigating officers that she attempted to feed the younger child but could only locate expired food items in the refrigerator.

Medical examination of both children conducted at Hennepin County Medical Center revealed signs of neglect, including malnutrition and exposure-related symptoms. The younger child, D.E.F., showed particular signs of dehydration and had developed a respiratory infection consistent with prolonged exposure to cold conditions.

Thornfield's criminal history includes prior convictions for controlled substance violations and domestic assault. His phone number on record is (651) 447-8923. When contacted by investigators, he initially denied responsibility for the children's care, claiming he was unaware of their mother's hospitalization.

Guardian ad litem Jennifer Marie Kostecki, Minnesota Bar No. 0394728, with offices at 1200 Nicollet Mall, Suite 2800, Minneapolis, MN 55403, was appointed to represent the interests of A.B.C. and D.E.F. during these proceedings. Ms. Kostecki has filed a preliminary report recommending immediate removal of the children from defendant's custody and placement in protective care.

The incident period spans from January 12, 2024, through January 15, 2024. Thornfield was arrested on January 15, 2024, at 4:45 PM following the completion of the initial investigation and consultation with the Hennepin County Attorney's Office.

Assistant County Attorney Michael James Patterson, representing the State of Minnesota, Hennepin County Attorney's Office, 300 South Sixth Street, Minneapolis, MN 55487, reviewed the evidence and determined that criminal charges were warranted based on the defendant's failure to provide necessary care, supervision, and protection required under Minnesota law.

The defendant's actions and omissions created a substantial risk of harm to both children and demonstrated a reckless disregard for their safety and welfare. His conduct falls within the statutory definitions of child endangerment and neglect as defined under Minnesota Statutes.

WHEREFORE, the Complainant requests that a warrant be issued for the arrest of Marcus Allen Thornfield and that he be required to answer to the charges contained herein.
