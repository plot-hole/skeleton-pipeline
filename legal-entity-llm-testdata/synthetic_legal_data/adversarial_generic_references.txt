**IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF COOK COUNTY, ILLINOIS**
**CRIMINAL DIVISION**

**THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF ILLINOIS,**
**Plaintiff,**

**v.**                                           **Case No. 2023-CR-45892**

**MARCUS DANIEL RODRIGUEZ,**

**SENTENCING ORDER**

This matter came before the Court on November 15, 2023, for sentencing of the defendant Marcus Daniel Rodriguez following his conviction by jury trial. The Court having reviewed the presentence investigation report, having heard arguments from the State and defense counsel, and having considered all relevant factors under the applicable sentencing guidelines, now enters the following Order.

**BACKGROUND AND PROCEDURAL HISTORY**

The defendant Marcus Daniel Rodriguez was arrested on March 8, 2023, and charged with aggravated battery in violation of 720 ILCS 5/12-3.05. The charges arose from an incident at the defendant's residence located at 4728 North Sheridan Road, Chicago, Illinois 60640. Following a jury trial conducted before this Court, the defendant Rodriguez was found guilty on all counts.

The Court notes that throughout these proceedings, the People have consistently advocated for a substantial period of incarceration, while defense counsel has argued for alternative sentencing options. The Court has carefully weighed the arguments presented by both the State and the defense in reaching its determination.

**SENTENCING FACTORS**

In determining the appropriate sentence for the defendant, the Court has considered the factors set forth in 730 ILCS 5/5-4.1-4. The Court finds that the nature and circumstances of the offense are serious, as the People established through clear and convincing evidence that the defendant's actions caused substantial bodily harm to the victim. The Court further finds that the defendant's criminal history, while limited, demonstrates a pattern of escalating behavior that the State has properly brought to this Court's attention.

The Court has also considered the impact of this offense on the victim and the community at large. The People presented compelling victim impact testimony that demonstrated the lasting effects of the defendant's criminal conduct. The Court acknowledges the Commonwealth's position that deterrence is a significant factor that must be weighed in fashioning an appropriate sentence.

The presentence investigation report indicates that the defendant has maintained employment and family support, factors which this Court views favorably. However, the Court must balance these mitigating circumstances against the severity of the offense and the need to protect the public safety, as the Government has properly emphasized throughout these proceedings.

**THE COURT'S FINDINGS**

After careful consideration of all relevant factors, the Court makes the following findings:

First, the Court finds that the defendant's conduct on the date in question was serious and warrants a substantial penalty. The People proved beyond a reasonable doubt that the defendant acted with intent to cause bodily harm, and the evidence established that substantial injury resulted from his actions.

Second, the Court finds that while the defendant has expressed remorse, the seriousness of the offense requires a sentence that reflects the gravity of his conduct. The State has correctly noted that this Court must consider not only rehabilitation but also deterrence and public safety.

Third, the Court finds that the defendant would benefit from counseling and anger management programs, which the Commonwealth has indicated are available through the Department of Corrections. The Court believes that such programming, combined with a period of incarceration, will serve both the defendant's rehabilitation needs and the public interest.

**SENTENCE**

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that the defendant Marcus Daniel Rodriguez is sentenced as follows:

The Court sentences the defendant to a term of imprisonment of four (4) years in the Illinois Department of Corrections. This Court finds that this sentence appropriately balances the need for punishment and deterrence with the possibility of rehabilitation.

The Court further orders that the defendant shall receive credit for time served in the Cook County Jail from the date of his arrest. The State has stipulated that the defendant has been continuously incarcerated since March 8, 2023.

The Court also orders that upon release from the Department of Corrections, the defendant shall be placed on supervised release for a period of two (2) years under the supervision of the Illinois Department of Corrections, Division of Community Services. The conditions of supervised release shall be as determined by that department in accordance with standard practices, and the Court retains jurisdiction to modify such conditions as circumstances may warrant.

**ADDITIONAL ORDERS**

The Court orders that the defendant shall pay court costs in the amount of $250, which the State has requested and which this Court finds appropriate under the circumstances. The Court further orders that the defendant shall pay restitution to the victim in an amount to be determined at a separate restitution hearing, which the Commonwealth shall schedule within sixty (60) days of this Order.

The Court advises the defendant that he has the right to appeal this sentence to the Illinois Appellate Court within thirty (30) days of the entry of this Order. If the defendant is indigent, the State will provide appellate counsel at no cost to him.

The Court notes that this sentence reflects careful consideration of all factors presented by both the People and the defense. The Court believes that this sentence serves the interests of justice while providing the defendant with an opportunity for rehabilitation and eventual reintegration into society.

The Court directs the Clerk to provide copies of this Order to the People, defense counsel, the Department of Corrections, and the Department of Community Services.

IT IS SO ORDERED.

ENTERED this 15th day of November, 2023.
