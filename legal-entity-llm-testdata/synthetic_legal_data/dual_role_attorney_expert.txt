**FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS**

11. In March 2022, the Plaintiff, through its Managing Member Sarah Elizabeth Henley, engaged Berkshire Financial Advisors to manage a portfolio of securities valued at approximately $2.3 million.

12. The Managing Member had extensive discussions with Rutherford regarding Meridian Capital's investment objectives, which focused on capital preservation and moderate growth suitable for a venture capital firm's reserve funds.

13. She specifically instructed that no more than 20% of the portfolio should be allocated to high-risk investments, and that liquidity should be maintained for potential investment opportunities.

14. Defendant Rutherford assured Ms. Henley that he understood these parameters and would manage the portfolio accordingly. The witness later acknowledged in depositions taken in related proceedings that he was fully aware of these restrictions.

15. Between April 2022 and September 2023, Rutherford, acting as Berkshire Financial's agent, repeatedly violated these instructions by allocating over 60% of Meridian Capital's assets to speculative cryptocurrency investments and leveraged derivatives.

16. When the Plaintiff questioned these allocations in July 2023, Defendant Rutherford provided misleading statements about the risk profile of the investments, claiming they were "conservative growth instruments with minimal downside exposure."

17. The witness further represented that the investments were "fully liquid" and could be converted to cash within 24-48 hours if needed.

18. Colonial Trust Company served as custodian for the account and had actual knowledge of the unauthorized high-risk allocations but failed to notify Plaintiff despite contractual obligations to monitor investment activities for compliance with client directives.

19. In October 2023, market volatility caused the speculative positions to lose approximately $1.4 million in value over a three-week period.

20. When Ms. Henley attempted to liquidate positions to cover a time-sensitive investment opportunity, she discovered that the investments could not be readily converted to cash due to market conditions and contractual restrictions that Rutherford had failed to disclose.

21. As a result of Defendants' breaches of fiduciary duty and misrepresentations, Meridian Capital suffered damages in excess of $1.4 million and lost profitable investment opportunities.

**COUNT I - BREACH OF FIDUCIARY DUTY**

22. Plaintiff incorporates by reference all preceding paragraphs.

23. Defendants owed fiduciary duties to Plaintiff, including duties of loyalty, care, and good faith.

24. Defendants breached these duties by making unauthorized high-risk investments contrary to explicit instructions and by failing to provide accurate information about investment risks and liquidity.

25. As a direct and proximate result of these breaches, Plaintiff suffered substantial monetary damages.
