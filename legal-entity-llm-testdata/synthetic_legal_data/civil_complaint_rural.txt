CIVIL COMPLAINT FOR PERSONAL INJURY

TO THE DEFENDANTS ABOVE NAMED:

Plaintiff Walter Eugene Harrison, by and through his attorneys, Bradley K. Thornton and Jennifer L. Murphy of Thornton & Associates, LLC, brings this action against defendants Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc. and Ronald Mitchell Santos, and in support thereof alleges as follows:

PARTIES

1. Plaintiff Walter Eugene Harrison is an individual residing at 4872 Township Road 315, Baltic, Ohio 43804, and is a lifelong resident of Stark County, Ohio. Harrison is a grain and livestock farmer who operates a 240-acre farm that has been in his family for three generations.

2. Defendant Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 1847 Industrial Boulevard, Toledo, Ohio 43615. At all times relevant hereto, Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc. was engaged in the business of interstate trucking and freight transportation.

3. Defendant Ronald Mitchell Santos is an individual residing at 9234 Maple Grove Drive, Akron, Ohio 44313. Santos was at all relevant times employed as a commercial truck driver by defendant Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc.

JURISDICTION AND VENUE

4. This Court has jurisdiction over this matter pursuant to Ohio Revised Code Section 2305.01, as the amount in controversy exceeds the jurisdictional limits of lower courts.

5. Venue is proper in Stark County pursuant to Ohio Civil Rule 3(B)(3), as the cause of action arose within this county.

FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

6. On March 17, 2023, at approximately 2:45 PM, plaintiff Harrison was operating his 2019 Ford F-350 pickup truck westbound on State Route 39, approximately two miles east of the village of Baltic, Ohio.

7. At the same time, defendant Santos was operating a 2021 Peterbilt tractor-trailer owned by defendant Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc., traveling eastbound on the same roadway.

8. As Harrison approached a slight curve in the roadway, defendant Santos's vehicle suddenly crossed the center line and entered plaintiff's lane of travel.

9. Despite Harrison's immediate evasive actions, including steering toward the shoulder and applying his brakes, Santos's tractor-trailer struck plaintiff's vehicle head-on.

10. The collision occurred due to defendant Santos's negligent operation of his commercial vehicle, including but not limited to: failing to maintain proper control of his vehicle, driving while fatigued in violation of federal hours-of-service regulations, and failing to keep a proper lookout.

11. At the time of the collision, defendant Santos had been driving for eleven consecutive hours without the required rest break, in direct violation of 49 C.F.R. § 395.8.

INJURIES AND DAMAGES

12. As a direct and proximate result of the collision, plaintiff Harrison sustained severe and permanent injuries, including: multiple rib fractures, a collapsed lung, cervical spine compression fractures at C4-C6, traumatic brain injury with ongoing cognitive impairment, and lacerations requiring surgical repair.

13. Harrison was transported by LifeFlight helicopter to Cleveland Clinic Akron General Hospital, where he remained hospitalized for seventeen days in the intensive care unit and trauma ward.

14. Plaintiff's medical treatment has included multiple surgeries performed by Dr. Marcus Alexander Kowalski, Chief of Orthopedic Surgery at Cleveland Clinic Akron General Hospital, who will testify as an expert witness regarding the extent and permanency of Harrison's injuries.

15. As a result of his injuries, Harrison has incurred medical expenses in excess of $247,000, including emergency treatment, surgical procedures, hospitalization, rehabilitation therapy, and ongoing medical care.

16. Plaintiff continues to suffer from chronic pain, limited mobility, and cognitive difficulties that prevent him from performing the physical demands of farming.

17. Harrison's injuries have resulted in a substantial loss of income, as he has been unable to operate his farm at full capacity since the accident, resulting in lost crop revenues and livestock sales totaling approximately $89,000 annually.

18. The plaintiff's pain and suffering, both past and future, is significant and ongoing, as confirmed by Dr. Kowalski's medical evaluation and prognosis.

COUNT ONE - NEGLIGENCE
(Against Defendant Santos)

19. Plaintiff realleges and incorporates by reference paragraphs 1 through 18 above.

20. Defendant Santos owed a duty to operate his commercial vehicle in a reasonable and prudent manner and in compliance with applicable traffic laws and federal regulations.

21. Santos breached this duty by operating his vehicle in a negligent manner as set forth above.

22. As a direct and proximate result of Santos's negligence, Harrison sustained the injuries and damages described herein.

COUNT TWO - RESPONDEAT SUPERIOR
(Against Defendant Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc.)

23. Plaintiff realleges and incorporates by reference paragraphs 1 through 22 above.

24. At the time of the collision, defendant Santos was acting within the course and scope of his employment with defendant Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc.

25. Midwest Freight Logistics, Inc. is vicariously liable for the negligent acts of its employee, Santos, under the doctrine of respondeat superior.

WHEREFORE, plaintiff Walter Eugene Harrison respectfully requests this Court grant judgment in his favor and against defendants, jointly and severally, in an amount in excess of $850,000, together with costs, pre- and post-judgment interest, and such other relief as this Court deems just and proper.

JURY DEMAND

Plaintiff hereby demands trial by jury on all issues so triable.
