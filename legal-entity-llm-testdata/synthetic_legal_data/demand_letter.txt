This firm represents Mr. David Andrew Castellanos in connection with injuries he sustained on October 12, 2023, at the Westfield Shopping Plaza located at 4820 Broadway Street, Boulder, Colorado 80304, which is owned and managed by your company, Mountain Vista Property Management Corp.

**FACTUAL BACKGROUND**

On the aforementioned date, Mr. Castellanos was lawfully present as an invitee at the shopping center when he suffered a severe slip-and-fall accident near the main entrance to the Macy's department store. The incident occurred at approximately 2:30 p.m. during normal business hours. Our client was walking toward the store entrance when he slipped on an accumulation of water that had collected on the walkway due to a malfunctioning overhead sprinkler system.

Mr. Castellanos, age 34, residing at 925 Maple Grove Lane, Boulder, Colorado 80301, telephone (720) 555-3249, date of birth September 18, 1989, sustained significant injuries as a direct and proximate result of your property's dangerous condition. The plaintiff fell backward, striking his head on the concrete walkway and severely injuring his lower back and left shoulder.

**INJURIES AND DAMAGES**

As a result of this incident, Mr. Castellanos has incurred substantial medical expenses and continues to suffer from his injuries. He was immediately transported by ambulance to Boulder Community Hospital, where emergency room physicians diagnosed him with a severe concussion, herniated discs at L4-L5 and L5-S1, and a torn rotator cuff in his left shoulder.

The plaintiff's medical treatment has included emergency room care, multiple MRI scans, extensive physical therapy, pain management injections, and ongoing orthopedic treatment. His treating physicians include Dr. Sarah Martinez at Boulder Orthopedic Associates and Dr. Robert Chen at Front Range Pain Management. Mr. Castellanos underwent arthroscopic shoulder surgery on December 8, 2023, and may require additional surgical intervention for his spinal injuries.

To date, our client's medical expenses total Forty-Seven Thousand Three Hundred Twenty-Two Dollars and 18/100 ($47,322.18). He has been unable to work at his position as a construction supervisor with Alpine Building Solutions since the date of injury, resulting in lost wages exceeding $28,000. His treating physicians have indicated that he may never return to his previous level of physical capacity, creating substantial future economic losses.

Beyond the economic damages, Mr. Castellanos has endured significant pain and suffering, loss of enjoyment of life, and permanent physical impairment. The plaintiff, who was previously an active individual who enjoyed hiking and skiing, can no longer participate in these activities due to his ongoing physical limitations.

**LIABILITY**

Mountain Vista Property Management Corp. maintained exclusive control over the premises where this incident occurred. Your company had both actual and constructive notice of the dangerous condition that caused our client's fall. Security camera footage obtained through our investigation shows that water had been accumulating in the area for at least thirty minutes prior to the accident, and several other pedestrians had navigated around the hazardous area.

The malfunctioning sprinkler system had been reported to building maintenance on multiple prior occasions, yet no adequate repairs were made. This constitutes a clear breach of your duty to maintain the premises in a reasonably safe condition for business invitees like Mr. Castellanos. Your negligent maintenance and failure to remedy the known hazard directly caused his injuries.
