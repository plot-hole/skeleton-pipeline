FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

6. On March 15, 2024, Wellington contracted with Court Services International, Inc. for comprehensive property management services for his residential investment property located at 500 Lake Shore Drive, a $5 million property in Springfield, Illinois.

7. The written agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit No. 1 and incorporated by reference, required Court Services International, Inc. to provide monthly inspections, maintenance coordination, and tenant screening services for the property.

8. Case, as Regional Manager, was personally responsible for overseeing Wellington's account and ensuring compliance with the service agreement terms outlined in Exhibit No. 2.

9. Between April 1, 2024, and September 30, 2024, defendants failed to perform the contracted services. Specifically, Case failed to conduct required monthly inspections as documented in Exhibit No. 3, failed to coordinate necessary repairs as shown in Exhibit No. 4, and improperly screened tenants resulting in significant property damage as evidenced by Exhibit No. 5.

10. As a direct result of defendants' negligence and breach of contract, Wellington suffered damages including lost rental income of $45,000, property repair costs of $125,000, and additional expenses totaling $30,000, all as detailed in the financial records attached as Exhibit No. 6.

11. Wellington made numerous attempts to contact Case directly at his office phone (217) 555-0456 and Court Services International, Inc.'s main line to resolve these issues, but defendants failed to adequately respond or remedy their performance failures.

12. The damage reports and correspondence documenting Wellington's attempts to resolve this matter are attached as Exhibit No. 7.

FIRST COUNT - BREACH OF CONTRACT

13. Wellington realleges and incorporates paragraphs 1 through 12 as if fully set forth herein.

14. A valid contract existed between Wellington and Court Services International, Inc., with Case acting as the company's authorized representative.

15. Wellington performed all conditions required under the contract, including timely payment of service fees totaling $15,000.

16. Defendants materially breached the contract by failing to provide the agreed-upon property management services, resulting in substantial damages to Wellington's property and business interests.

17. As a proximate result of defendants' breach, Wellington has suffered damages in excess of $200,000.

SECOND COUNT - NEGLIGENCE

18. Wellington realleges and incorporates paragraphs 1 through 17 as if fully set forth herein.

19. Defendants owed Wellington a duty to exercise reasonable care in providing property management services and to conduct business operations in accordance with industry standards.

20. Case and Court Services International, Inc. breached their duty of care by failing to properly inspect the property, failing to coordinate necessary maintenance, and failing to adequately screen tenants.

21. Defendants' negligent conduct was the proximate cause of Wellington's damages, including property damage, lost income, and additional expenses incurred to mitigate the harm caused by their failures.
