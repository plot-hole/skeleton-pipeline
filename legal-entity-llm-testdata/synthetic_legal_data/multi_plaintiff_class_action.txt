FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

10. Between January 2022 and December 2023, defendants engaged in a fraudulent scheme to market and sell dietary supplements containing materially false and misleading representations regarding their health benefits and ingredient composition.

11. VitaMax manufactured and distributed "PowerBoost Energy Formula" supplements, claiming the products contained "clinically proven herbal extracts" and would "increase energy levels by 300% within 30 days."

12. Rodriguez purchased PowerBoost Energy Formula from SuperMart in Sacramento on March 15, 2023, relying on the representations made by VitaMax and promoted by SuperMart.

13. Thompson bought the same product from a SuperMart location in Los Angeles on June 8, 2023, after seeing advertising materials that he now knows contained false claims.

14. Williams purchased PowerBoost Energy Formula on August 22, 2023, from SuperMart in San Diego, believing the product would deliver the promised health benefits.

15. Chen acquired the supplements from SuperMart in Fresno on October 5, 2023, after reviewing marketing materials that misrepresented the product's effectiveness.

16. Independent laboratory testing revealed that PowerBoost Energy Formula contained none of the "clinically proven herbal extracts" advertised by defendants, but instead consisted primarily of caffeine and common vitamins available at a fraction of the retail price.

17. Sterling, as CEO of VitaMax, personally approved the false marketing campaign and directed the company's advertising department to make unsubstantiated health claims. He knew or should have known that the representations were materially false.

18. SuperMart actively promoted and endorsed these false claims through in-store displays, promotional materials, and employee training programs that reinforced the deceptive messaging.

CLASS ACTION ALLEGATIONS

19. Plaintiffs bring this action on behalf of themselves and all others similarly situated, specifically: "All persons in California who purchased PowerBoost Energy Formula between January 1, 2022, and December 31, 2023."

20. The class is so numerous that joinder of all members is impracticable, as defendants sold over 250,000 units of the product during the relevant time period.

21. On February 14, 2024, this Court granted plaintiffs' motion for class certification, finding that common questions of law and fact predominate over individual issues.

22. Rodriguez, Thompson, Williams, and Chen reallege and incorporate by reference all preceding paragraphs.

23. Defendants' conduct constitutes unfair, unlawful, and fraudulent business practices in violation of California Business and Professions Code Section 17200 et seq.

COUNT II - NEGLIGENT MISREPRESENTATION

24. Plaintiffs reallege all preceding allegations.

25. Sterling and VitaMax made material misrepresentations regarding the composition and effectiveness of PowerBoost Energy Formula with insufficient investigation into the truth of such claims.

COUNT III - FRAUD AND DECEIT

26. All previous allegations are incorporated herein.

27. Defendants intentionally misrepresented material facts regarding the product's ingredients and benefits, knowing such representations were false, with the intent that consumers would rely on them.
