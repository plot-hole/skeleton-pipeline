SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

This Settlement Agreement and Release ("Agreement") is entered into this 15th day of March, 2024, between Marcus Jonathan Hensley, an individual with Social Security Number 247-89-3156, residing at 847 Maple Ridge Drive, Cherry Hill, New Jersey 08034, telephone (856) 442-7891, email mhensley.personal@gmail.com ("Claimant" or "Mr. Hensley"), and TechnoMed Solutions Corporation, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2200 Corporate Center Boulevard, Suite 450, Austin, Texas 78731, Employer Identification Number 74-3892617 ("Defendant" or "Company").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, on August 22, 2022, Claimant alleges he sustained serious injuries while using Defendant's Model X-7400 Advanced Infusion Pump at Memorial Healthcare Center in Mount Laurel, New Jersey;

WHEREAS, Mr. Hensley filed suit against TechnoMed Solutions Corporation in the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey, Civil Action No. 2023-CV-04782, alleging product liability, negligence, and breach of warranty claims;

WHEREAS, the Claimant is represented by Rebecca Martinez-Chen, Esq., of Martinez & Associates LLP, located at 1800 Market Street, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19103;

WHEREAS, Defendant is represented by its General Counsel, David Andrew Kowalski, Esq., email dkowalski@technomed-solutions.com, direct telephone (512) 889-4523;

WHEREAS, the parties participated in mediation conducted by Hon. Patricia L. Brennan (Ret.) on February 8, 2024, at her offices in Princeton, New Jersey;

WHEREAS, the parties wish to resolve all claims and avoid the uncertainty and expense of continued litigation;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. SETTLEMENT PAYMENT

Defendant agrees to pay Claimant the total sum of One Million Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($1,400,000.00) in settlement of all claims. Payment shall be made according to the following schedule:

a) Initial payment of Three Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($350,000.00) within fifteen (15) days of execution of this Agreement;
b) Second payment of Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($400,000.00) on or before June 15, 2024;
c) Third payment of Three Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($325,000.00) on or before September 15, 2024;
d) Final payment of Three Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($325,000.00) on or before December 15, 2024.

All payments shall be made by certified check or wire transfer to Marcus Jonathan Hensley or his designated account.

2. RELEASE OF CLAIMS

In consideration for the payments described above, Mr. Hensley, on behalf of himself, his heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns, hereby releases and forever discharges TechnoMed Solutions Corporation, its parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, officers, directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns from any and all claims, demands, damages, actions, causes of action, or suits of any kind arising from or related to the incident of August 22, 2022, or the use of the Model X-7400 Advanced Infusion Pump. This release shall become effective on April 1, 2024.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY

The Claimant agrees that the terms and conditions of this Agreement, including the settlement amount, shall remain strictly confidential. He shall not disclose any information regarding this settlement to any third parties, except as required by law or to his immediate family, accountants, or tax advisors who agree to maintain confidentiality. Mr. Hensley acknowledges that breach of this confidentiality provision may result in monetary damages to Defendant.

4. NO ADMISSION OF LIABILITY

This Agreement and the settlement payment do not constitute an admission of wrongdoing, fault, or liability by TechnoMed Solutions Corporation. The Company specifically denies all allegations of defective design, manufacturing defects, or inadequate warnings related to the Model X-7400 device.

5. DISMISSAL OF LAWSUIT

Within ten (10) days of execution of this Agreement, Claimant's counsel, Rebecca Martinez-Chen, shall file a stipulation of dismissal with prejudice in Civil Action No. 2023-CV-04782 in the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey.

6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Marcus Jonathan Hensley represents that he has not assigned or transferred any claims related to this matter to any third party. He further represents that he has full authority to enter into this Agreement and that his execution hereof has been duly authorized.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to conflict of law principles.

8. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements relating to the subject matter hereof.
