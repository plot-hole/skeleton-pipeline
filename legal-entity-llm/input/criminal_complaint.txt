**CRIMINAL COMPLAINT**

I, Detective Raymond Charles Blackwood, Badge No. 4729, being duly sworn, state that I have reason to believe that on or about the dates between January 15, 2023, and September 8, 2023, in Wayne County, within the Eastern District of Michigan, Marcus Wellington Hayes, SSN 417-88-2936, did knowingly and willfully commit offenses against the United States, to wit:

**COUNT ONE**
Violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1343 (Wire Fraud)

From January 15, 2023, through September 8, 2023, the defendant Marcus Wellington Hayes did devise and intend to devise a scheme and artifice to defraud, and to obtain money and property by means of false and fraudulent pretenses, representations, and promises, and for the purpose of executing said scheme, did transmit and cause to be transmitted by means of wire communication in interstate commerce, writings, signs, signals, pictures and sounds, contrary to 18 U.S.C. § 1343.

**COUNT TWO**
Violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1341 (Mail Fraud)

During the same time period, Hayes did knowingly devise a scheme to defraud and obtain money by means of false pretenses, and placed materials in the United States mail system for the purpose of executing said fraudulent scheme, in violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1341.

**STATEMENT OF FACTS**

The investigation conducted by this affiant reveals the following facts:

Marcus Wellington Hayes, age 42, residing at 1847 Corktown Avenue, Detroit, Michigan 48216, operated a fraudulent investment scheme targeting elderly residents throughout southeastern Michigan. Hayes presented himself as a licensed financial advisor and convinced victims to invest in what he claimed were "high-yield municipal bonds backed by federal guarantees."

The primary victim in this case is Margaret Catherine Thornfield, age 67, of 2904 Maple Ridge Drive, Birmingham, Michigan 48009. Mrs. Thornfield was contacted by Hayes via telephone at (248) 555-0147 on January 15, 2023. During this initial contact, the defendant represented that he was a certified financial planner with over twenty years of experience managing portfolios for retirees.

Hayes provided Mrs. Thornfield with fabricated documentation, including falsified prospectuses and fraudulent certificates of deposit. He instructed her to wire transfer funds to a business account he controlled under the name "Premier Municipal Securities LLC." Between January 22, 2023, and August 14, 2023, Thornfield transferred a total of $247,800 to accounts controlled by the defendant.

The investigation revealed that Hayes used the email address mhayespremier@tempmail.com to communicate with victims and send falsified investment documents. He would contact victims initially by telephone from his personal number (313) 555-0892, then direct them to communicate via email to avoid creating phone records.

A minor witness, identified only by initials J.T.M., age 15, observed Hayes at the defendant's residence on multiple occasions during the summer of 2023. The minor witness reported seeing Hayes operating multiple computers and observed him discussing "bond payments" and "investor returns" during phone calls. This witness's testimony provided crucial timeline evidence linking Hayes to the fraudulent activities at his Corktown Avenue address.

Detective Blackwood's investigation, initiated on September 12, 2023, following a complaint filed by Margaret Catherine Thornfield, uncovered evidence of Hayes's scheme through bank records, email communications, and witness interviews. Financial records obtained through court-authorized searches revealed that Hayes had diverted victim funds to personal accounts and used the money for gambling, luxury purchases, and personal expenses rather than legitimate investments.

The scheme unraveled when Mrs. Thornfield attempted to withdraw funds in August 2023 and Hayes provided increasingly evasive responses to her inquiries. She contacted the Michigan Department of Insurance and Financial Services, which determined that neither Hayes nor "Premier Municipal Securities LLC" held valid licenses to offer investment services in Michigan.

Hayes was arrested on October 3, 2023, at his residence by members of the FBI Financial Crimes Task Force and Detroit Police Department. During the arrest, agents seized multiple computers, cell phones, and documents related to the fraudulent investment scheme.

The total amount defrauded from Margaret Catherine Thornfield was $247,800. Investigation continues into additional potential victims who may have been targeted by Hayes during this period.

This complaint is filed by Patricia Marie Rodriguez, Assistant United States Attorney, United States Attorney's Office, 211 West Fort Street, Suite 2001, Detroit, Michigan 48226, on November 8, 2023.

The defendant's scheme violated federal wire fraud statutes by using interstate wire communications to perpetrate fraud, and violated mail fraud statutes by utilizing the United States Postal Service to deliver fraudulent materials to victims.

Based on the foregoing facts, I respectfully request that a warrant be issued for the arrest of Marcus Wellington Hayes for violations of 18 U.S.C. § 1343 and 18 U.S.C. § 1341.
