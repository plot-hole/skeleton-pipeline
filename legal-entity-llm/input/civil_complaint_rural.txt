FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

8. On March 15, 2023, at approximately 2:47 p.m., Plaintiff Thomas Eugene Whitfield was operating his 2018 Ford F-250 pickup truck in a westerly direction on State Route 9, approximately two miles east of Cadiz, Ohio.

9. At the same time and place, Defendant Rodriguez was operating a commercial freight truck owned by Defendant Midwest Freight Solutions, Inc. in an easterly direction on the same roadway.

10. While Whitfield was lawfully operating his vehicle within his designated lane of travel, Defendant Rodriguez negligently and carelessly operated his commercial vehicle, causing it to cross the center line and enter Plaintiff's lane of travel.

11. As a direct and proximate result of Rodriguez's negligent operation of the commercial vehicle, a head-on collision occurred between the commercial truck and Whitfield's pickup truck.

12. The impact caused severe and permanent injuries to Plaintiff, including but not limited to: multiple fractures of the left femur, traumatic brain injury, herniated discs at L4-L5 and L5-S1, and extensive soft tissue damage.

13. As a result of his injuries, Whitfield has required extensive medical treatment, including emergency surgery performed by Dr. Jonathan K. Matthews, an orthopedic surgeon affiliated with East Ohio Regional Medical Center.

14. Plaintiff's medical expenses to date total $347,892.67, and he will require ongoing medical treatment and rehabilitation for the remainder of his life.

15. The injuries sustained by Whitfield have prevented him from operating his 240-acre farming operation, resulting in substantial lost income and diminished earning capacity.

16. At the time of the incident, Defendant Rodriguez was acting within the course and scope of his employment with Defendant Midwest Freight Solutions, Inc.

17. Defendants' insurance coverage is provided by Continental Commercial Insurance Company, Policy Number CC-2847691-OH.

COUNT ONE - NEGLIGENCE
(Against Defendant Rodriguez)

18. Plaintiff incorporates by reference all preceding paragraphs as if fully rewritten herein.

19. Defendant Rodriguez owed a duty to Plaintiff Whitfield to operate his commercial vehicle in a reasonable and prudent manner in accordance with applicable traffic laws and regulations.

20. Rodriguez breached this duty by failing to maintain proper control of his vehicle, crossing the center line, and entering Plaintiff's lane of travel.

21. As a direct and proximate result of Rodriguez's breach of duty, Whitfield sustained severe and permanent injuries as described herein.

COUNT TWO - RESPONDEAT SUPERIOR
(Against Defendant Midwest Freight Solutions, Inc.)

22. Plaintiff incorporates by reference all preceding paragraphs as if fully rewritten herein.

23. At the time of the incident, Defendant Rodriguez was acting as an agent and employee of Defendant Midwest Freight Solutions, Inc., and was acting within the course and scope of his employment.

24. Midwest Freight Solutions, Inc. is therefore liable for the negligent acts of its employee Rodriguez under the doctrine of respondeat superior.
